# Neighborhood Deep-Dives

Beyond the major attractions, these neighborhoods offer the soul of Tokyo. Each has a distinct personality and makes for an excellent half-day exploration.

---

## 🏮 Yanaka — Old Tokyo Time Capsule

**Station:** Nippori (JR Yamanote) or Sendagi (Chiyoda Line)

One of the few Tokyo neighborhoods that survived WWII bombing. Old wooden houses, narrow lanes, temple-dotted streets, and cats everywhere. This is "shitamachi" (old downtown) — feels like stepping back 60 years.

### Walking Route (~2 hours)

1. Start at **Nezu Station** and walk to **Nezu Shrine** — one of Tokyo's oldest and most beautiful, with a tunnel of vermilion torii gates
2. Continue north through the quiet temple-lined streets towards Yanaka
3. Walk along the edge of **Yanaka Cemetery** — atmospheric and lined with cherry trees
4. Explore the narrow residential lanes, old wooden houses and tiny shrines
5. Finish at **Yanaka Ginza** shopping street once its small shops and food counters are open
6. Try **menchi-katsu** (deep-fried meat patties) and browse the local shops

### Where to Eat

- **Yanaka Beer Hall** — craft beer in a renovated house
- **Kayaba Coffee** — renovated 1916 building, famous egg sandwich and "Russian coffee" (with whipped cream)
- **Himitsu-do** — famous kakigori (shaved ice). Expect a line, worth the wait
- **Nezu no Taiyaki** — fish-shaped waffle with bean paste

### Best Paired With

Ueno (walk from Ueno through Yanaka to Nippori, 30 min). The Yanaka-Nezu-Sendagi walking route is one of Tokyo's best half-day strolls.

## 🎸 Shimokitazawa — Bohemian Tokyo

**Station:** Shimokitazawa (Odakyu Line, Keio Inokashira Line — 3 min from Shibuya)

Tokyo's indie/bohemian neighborhood. Vintage clothing, independent theaters, live music, quirky cafes, record stores. The anti-Shibuya. Compact and very walkable.

### Walking Route (~2-3 hours)

1. Exit **Shimokitazawa Station** south exit
2. Browse **vintage shops** along the main lanes — 2nd Street, Flamingo, New York Joe Exchange (high-quality vintage at reasonable prices)
3. Duck into **record stores** — everything from jazz to punk to obscure electronica
4. Visit **Bonus Track** — converted rail space with indie shops and cafes. A curated, laid-back vibe
5. Explore the curry shops — Shimokitazawa is a curry hotspot. Walk around and pick one that smells best
6. Check out the small live houses and theater posters — the neighborhood has a strong performing arts scene

### Where to Eat

- **Bear Pond Espresso** — legendary coffee, eccentric owner. No photos of the espresso machine allowed
- **Shirube** — tiny izakaya with great atmosphere
- Various curry shops — Shimokitazawa is curry central; wander Suzunari/Bonus Track lanes and pick one that smells best

### Best Paired With

Combine with a Shibuya day (3 min train ride). Can also combine with Koenji for a full "indie Tokyo" day.

## 🎵 Koenji — Punk & Counterculture

**Station:** Koenji (JR Chuo/Sobu Line — 7 min from Shinjuku)

Even more underground than Shimokitazawa. Punk, counterculture, thrift shops, live music with jazz/punk/experimental scenes. Less polished, more authentic. Home to the famous Awa Odori festival (August).

### Walking Route (~2 hours)

1. Exit **Koenji Station** south exit
2. Walk the **shotengai** (covered shopping arcades) — eclectic mix of old shops and new
3. Browse **Sokkyou** and **Hayatochiri** — vintage shops with rare finds at better prices than Shimokitazawa
4. Explore the **used record stores** — deep crates, obscure Japanese pressings
5. Walk the back streets — tiny bars, independent galleries, hand-painted signs
6. Check evening listings at **live houses** — intimate venues with ¥1,500-3,000 cover including a drink

### Where to Eat

- **Enso** — excellent ramen
- Various cheap izakayas with a locals-only vibe
- The shotengai has affordable standing-eat options

### Best Paired With

Combine with Shimokitazawa for a "vintage Tokyo" day, or add to a Shinjuku evening (7 min train).

## 🎌 Kagurazaka — French-Japanese Fusion District

**Station:** Kagurazaka (Tozai Line) or Iidabashi (JR, multiple subway lines)

Called "the little Paris of Tokyo" due to a French school and cultural institute nearby. But it's also one of Tokyo's old geisha districts — narrow cobblestone alleys ("kakurenbo yokocho" / "hide-and-seek alleys") hide traditional ryotei restaurants. A unique blend of French and Japanese culture.

### Walking Route (~1.5 hours)

1. Start at the bottom of **Kagurazaka slope** (from Iidabashi Station)
2. Walk up the main slope — shops, bakeries, restaurants on both sides
3. Turn into the **stone-paved side alleys** branching off — this is where the magic is. Get deliberately lost
4. Find **Akagi Shrine** — renovated by architect Kengo Kuma. Modern design meets ancient Shinto
5. Browse French bakeries mixed with Japanese craft shops
6. Explore the **ryotei alleys** — traditional high-end restaurants behind noren curtains

### Where to Eat

- **French bistros and bakeries** — Paul, Le Bretagne (crepes/galettes)
- **Traditional ryotei** along the hidden alleys (reservations needed for dinner)
- **Canal Cafe** — canal-side Italian/cafe with boat rentals. Lovely on a warm evening

### Best Paired With

Combine with Shinjuku day or Imperial Palace area. Works as a lunch destination between sights.

## 🌿 Tomigaya / Yoyogi-Uehara — Design-Conscious Local Tokyo

**Station:** Yoyogi-Uehara (Chiyoda Line) or Yoyogi-Koen (Chiyoda Line)

The quiet, trendy neighborhood just behind Shibuya and Yoyogi Park. Where design-conscious Tokyoites actually eat and drink. Excellent independent cafes, natural wine bars, and chef-driven small restaurants.

### What to Do

- **Cafe-hop** — this area has some of Tokyo's best
- **Fuglen Tokyo** — Norwegian specialty coffee by day, cocktail bar by night, in a gorgeous mid-century space
- **Camelback** — excellent egg sandwich from a tiny counter
- **Path** — popular bakery/restaurant
- **Ahiru Store** — legendary natural wine bar (arrive early, it's tiny and doesn't take reservations)
- Walk through **Yoyogi Park** — spacious, green, perfect for a break

### Best Paired With

Walkable from Harajuku/Shibuya. Perfect as an afternoon addition to a Meiji Shrine + Harajuku day.
